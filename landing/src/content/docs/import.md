---
title: Import drawings
description: Bring your drawings over from Excalidraw+ or from files on your computer.
---

## From Excalidraw+ (beta)

Open **Settings**, then **Import**, and paste an Excalidraw+ API key. Create
one in Excalidraw+ under workspace settings. Read access is enough. The key is
used for this import only and is not saved.

- **Admins** bring over scenes and images. Shared collections become folders
  in Team. A personal key (starts with `uk-`) also brings your private scenes,
  which go to Private.
- **Members** use a personal key to bring over their private scenes and images.
  They go to Private. An admin imports the shared collections.

An image that is too large or missing in Excalidraw+ does not come over. The
drawing shows a placeholder instead.

## From files

Add `.excalidraw` files from your computer under **Settings**, then **Import**.
They go to Private. PNG and SVG exports that include the scene work too.

To put files in a folder or in Team, drop them on it in the sidebar.
