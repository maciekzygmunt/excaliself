---
title: Share and collaborate
description: Edit a drawing together in real time, with members or with guests who have no account.
---

Everyone in a drawing sees the cursors and edits of the others as they happen.
Follow mode lets you watch a collaborator's view.

## Private drawings

Open a drawing and use **Share**. A drawing has one share link, and everyone
who opens it can edit, live. Choose who can open it:

| Audience | Who can open the link |
|---|---|
| **Members of this instance** | Visitors must sign in first. This is the default |
| **Anyone with the link** | No account needed. Guests pick a display name |

## Team drawings

Members already have access to [Team drawings](/docs/team-space/). The Share
dialog copies a link for them, and it can create a guest link for people outside
the instance. Anyone with a guest link can edit, live, with no account.

Stopping or regenerating a guest link sends only the guests away. Members stay
in the drawing.

## Manage a link

- **Stop sharing** deletes the link.
- **Regenerate link** replaces the link. The old URL stops working.

A dead link looks the same in every case. It does not reveal whether the drawing
exists.

## Limits

- A live drawing has 25 seats. One seat is kept for the owner.
- An image can be up to 2 MB, and a drawing can store up to 20 MB of images.
  Other people see a placeholder for an image that is refused.
- Guests do not see the [Team library](/docs/team-library/).
