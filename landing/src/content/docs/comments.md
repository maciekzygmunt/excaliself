---
title: Comments
description: Pin comment threads to a spot or a shape on a drawing, reply, and resolve them.
---

Members leave comments pinned to a spot on a drawing. Others reply in the
thread, and resolve it when it is done.

## Add a comment

1. Click **Comments** in the top-right corner of the drawing. On a narrow
   window, open the main menu and click **Comments**.
2. Click **Add comment**, then click a spot on the canvas.
3. Write your comment and press **Enter**. **Shift+Enter** adds a new line.

A comment on a shape stays on that shape when someone moves, resizes, or
rotates it. A comment on an empty part of the canvas stays on that spot.

If someone deletes the shape, the comment stays where the shape was, marked
**Shape deleted**. If they undo the delete, the comment goes back on the
shape.

## Read and reply

- Every member who can see the drawing sees its comment pins: every member
  for a Team drawing, only you for a Private one.
- Click a pin to open its thread and reply.
- The **Comments** panel lists every thread. Click one to move the canvas to
  it.
- New comments, replies, and resolves show up live in every open tab.
- A dot marks a thread with comments you have not read. The **Comments**
  button counts them.
- You can edit or delete your own comments. A thread goes away when its last
  comment is deleted.

## Resolve

Click the check mark on a thread to resolve it. Resolved threads are hidden
on the canvas. Turn on **Show resolved** in the panel to see them. To reply to
a resolved thread, reopen it first.

## Good to know

- Comments are not part of the drawing: image exports and `.excalidraw` files
  do not include them.
- A drawing in the trash keeps its comments, read-only. Restoring it brings
  them back. Deleting it for good deletes them.
- When a member is deleted, their comments on Team drawings stay, shown as
  **Former member**.
- Guests on a share link do not see comments.
- An AI client can read and write comments. See
  [Connect an AI client](/docs/mcp/#tools).
- A comment can be up to 2000 characters, as plain text. Web addresses become
  links.
