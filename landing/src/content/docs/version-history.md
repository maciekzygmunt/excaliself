---
title: Version history
description: Go back to an earlier version of a drawing.
---

Each drawing keeps its past versions. Open a drawing, then select
**Version history** in the menu.

## When a version is saved

- When you start to edit after a break of 10 minutes or more. The version is
  the drawing as it was before the break.
- Every 30 minutes while you or your team edit without a break.
- Versions are saved for Private and Team drawings, and for shared drawings.

A version that is the same as the one before it is not saved again. An empty
drawing has no versions.

## Restore a version

Select a version to see a preview. Then select **Restore as a copy**. The
version opens as a new drawing in your Private space, named
"*name* (restored)". The original drawing does not change.

Images in the version are restored too. They do not count toward the image
limit of the original drawing.

## Who can see the history

- A Private drawing: only you.
- A Team drawing: every member.
- Guests on a share link cannot see the history.

## Limits

- Each drawing keeps its last 50 versions. When a new version is saved, the
  oldest one is deleted.
- When you delete a drawing for good, its history is deleted too.
